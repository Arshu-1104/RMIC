# IDS per-call latency: before vs after the embedding cache

Generated by `experiment/perf/ids_latency.py`. Each call = `EnforcementEngine.evaluate_and_maybe_execute(..., enforcement_mode="ids_only", execute_tool=False)` on the first turn of every `prompts_v2` item (80 per contract), fresh session, real `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, after one untimed model-loading call. *Cold* = first call per contract (fills the per-contract caches); *warm* = the other 79. Wall-clock on one Windows laptop (CPU), single run; treat as indicative.

**Bit-identical scores:** yes — 3200 component values compared (10 components × 80 calls × 4 contracts), 0 differ.

| contract | cold before | cold after | warm median before | warm median after | warm p95 before | warm p95 after | speed-up (median) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| financial_agent | 65 ms | 7 ms | 610.7 ms | 8.2 ms | 781.8 ms | 14.4 ms | 74.9× |
| healthcare_research_agent | 923 ms | 40 ms | 676.6 ms | 8.4 ms | 872.0 ms | 11.8 ms | 80.9× |
| legal_review_agent | 871 ms | 27 ms | 706.4 ms | 77.3 ms | 884.2 ms | 106.6 ms | 9.1× |
| support_agent | 960 ms | 261 ms | 557.5 ms | 58.9 ms | 738.8 ms | 89.9 ms | 9.5× |

Note on the "after" column: across three runs legal_review_agent and support_agent (always measured third and fourth) showed 57–102 ms warm medians while the first two contracts showed ~8 ms. Measured in isolation in reverse order, all four contracts are 7.4–8.5 ms per warm call, of which ~6–7 ms is the single remaining ONNX embedding of the scored text; the in-run slowdown is a position effect on this machine (sustained load), not a property of those contracts. Expect ~8 ms per warm call here.
